## 3. Findings / things to verify

1. **2025 `total_lumi_pb` (110 840.0) ≠ sum of its per-era `lumi_pb` (110 850.0)** — a 10 pb⁻¹
   mismatch (`dataset_nanoAODv15_run3.yaml:575` vs `:582,596,608,620,634,646`). 2024 and 2026 are
   internally consistent. Confirm which value is authoritative before it feeds MC normalization.
2. **2026 luminosities are Preliminary Offline Results** (all four `lumi_pb` comments +
   `total_lumi_pb`). Update on final certification.
3. **2026 era C (low-PU, 2 110 pb⁻¹) is `skip_sample: True`** and intentionally omitted from the
   analysis and from `total_lumi_pb`. Its NANOAOD datasets are listed but unused.
4. **2025 & 2026 MC are 2024-conditions Summer24 simulation** — no 2025- or 2026-conditions central
   NanoAODv15 campaign exists yet (comments cite only private "forTSGStudies" test productions for a
   few DY/tt processes). Data/MC agreement for 2025/2026 relies on the (real, year-specific) JME
   corrections absorbing the conditions difference.
5. **`vbf_aMCatNLO` and `vbf_powheg` list a base + `_ext1` dataset** in all three years — confirm
   stage-1 `sumGenWgts`/normalization correctly combines base + ext1 for these.
6. **Muon PD count differs**: 2026 data uses `Muon0`–`Muon3`; 2024/2025 use `Muon0`,`Muon1`.
   Verify the prestage/DAS resolution handles the 4-PD case.
